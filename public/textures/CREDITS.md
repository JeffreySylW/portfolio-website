Planet looks (public/textures/planets/*.webp), baked by scripts/make-planet-textures.mjs:
- nasa.webp: built from the NASA insignia, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NASA_logo.svg (source on that page: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/themes/nasa/assets/images/nasa-logo.svg). Public domain for copyright, but NASA restricts use of its insignia (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/nasa-logo-use/, 14 CFR 1221). Jeff chose to use it for his own portfolio; permission can be requested at agency-brand@mail.nasa.gov. Not confirmed to match NASA's current master artwork exactly.
- education.webp: the VCU seal and wordmark lockup supplied by Jeff (scripts/assets/vcu-lockup.png), used unaltered; VCU's brand rules: https://brand.vcu.edu/vcu-university/guidelines/logos/primary-logo
- bob.webp, aspire.webp, ta.webp: drawn by the script (no third-party artwork).
